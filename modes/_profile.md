## Writing Style

**Tone:** Direct, confident, no hedging qualifiers
**Sentence length:** Short to medium, action-first
**Openings:** Action verbs — "Built", "Delivered", "Reduced", "Designed"
**Punctuation:** No em dashes in candidate-facing text. Oxford comma. No ellipses.
**Vocabulary:** "built" over "developed", "ran" over "managed", "cut" over "reduced"
**Structure:** Result-first, then method. One claim per sentence.
**Voice:** "I built", "I delivered" — first-person, active, no passive voice
**Avoid:** "passionate", "leveraged", "spearheaded", "results-oriented", "I would love the opportunity", abbreviations, shorthand, em dashes
